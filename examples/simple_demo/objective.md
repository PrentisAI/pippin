# Simple Demo Objective

Investigate surface segregation energetics of Zn in Cu(111) and its
effect on CO adsorption for methanol synthesis catalyst design.

## Key Questions
1. What is the segregation energy of Zn from bulk to Cu(111) surface?
2. How does Zn coverage affect ΔE_seg?
3. What is the effect of Zn segregation on CO binding?

## Expected Workflow
- Roundtable: 2 seats deliberate on approach
- Screening: Score the proposal
- Planning: Generate 3-job experiment plan
- Research loop: Execute structure → DFT → segregation analysis
- Analysis: Summarise findings
- Writing: Draft brief manuscript
