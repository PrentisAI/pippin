# Q4 (optional): Daily trips and weather - association only

**Data.** Daily weather for NYC Central Park (40.7829, -73.9654) from the Open-Meteo archive API (source URL, retrieval date and units in `tables/weather_source.json`; deg C, mm). Values are a gridded reanalysis at one point, used as a proxy for city-wide weather. 731 days (2023-01-01..2024-12-31), no missing values, joined 1:1 to the cleaned daily trip counts (79,371,599 trips; member + casual = total on every day).

**Findings (association, not causation).**
1. Daily trips rise with temperature: Spearman rho = 0.73 (95% moving-block-bootstrap CI 0.62 to 0.78). Casual riders 0.81 (0.72 to 0.84), members 0.66 (0.52 to 0.73). Weekdays 0.74 and weekends 0.73, so no clear weekday/weekend difference. Figure: `figures/fig_temp_vs_trips.png`.
2. Trips fall with precipitation: rho = -0.29 (-0.38 to -0.19). Adjusted for temperature and calendar, relative to dry days: 0-2.5 mm -9% (95% CI -11 to -6), 2.5-10 mm -23% (-27 to -19), >10 mm -42% (-48 to -36). Figure: `figures/fig_precip_vs_trips.png`.
3. The temperature association is nonlinear: adjusted +38% per +10 C between 10 and 20 C, but only about +11% per +10 C between 20 and 30 C (CI 3 to 19%); the fitted curve flattens near 29 C.
4. Much of the temperature association is shared with season. R2 of log trips: temperature alone 0.50; day-of-week + holiday + year 0.11; month + those 0.62; adding temperature (quadratic) and rain 0.80. With month fixed effects, temperature is identified from within-month day-to-day variation and the coefficient stays about +3.7% per C (95% CI 3.0 to 4.4%), similar to the unadjusted 3.7%.

**Controls used.** Log daily trips regressed on temperature (linear or quadratic), precipitation bins (reference: 0 mm), day-of-week, a US federal-holiday indicator and a year indicator; the sensitivity model adds month fixed effects. Newey-West HAC standard errors, 7 lags (14 lags raises the temperature SE from 0.0036 to 0.0042). Full table: `tables/weather_models.csv`; numbers: `tables/weather_numbers.json`.

**Limitations.** Season (daylight, school calendar, tourism) confounds temperature, and month fixed effects only partly remove it. One grid point stands in for the whole city, and daily precipitation totals lose timing within the day. Residuals remain autocorrelated (Durbin-Watson 1.15), so intervals may be somewhat narrow. Mean daily trips grew from about 96k (2023) to 121k (2024); the year indicator absorbs this as a level shift only. The Spearman CIs use block bootstrap with block length 14 days (10 observations for the weekday/weekend subsets, which are not contiguous series).

**Figure check.** Both PNGs were regenerated by code/04_weather.py from the 731-row joined table (temperature in deg C; trips per day in thousands). The precipitation panel shows bin counts (0: 367, 0-2.5: 180, 2.5-10: 90, >10: 94 days; weekday Apr-Oct panel 154/81/37/34). Bins are right-closed, so exactly 2.5 mm falls in the 0-2.5 bin. The earlier title claim about wet days being more common in some seasons was removed; computed from tables/weather_joined_daily.csv, the share of days with >2.5 mm precipitation is 27% (Dec-Feb, 49/181 days), 24% (Mar-May, 45/184), 32% (Jun-Aug, 59/184) and 17% (Sep-Nov, 31/182), so wet days are not concentrated in the cold months.
